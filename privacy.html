Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TIKLIVE handles information when you use the application.

1. Information and Permissions

TIKLIVE is being developed to support sign-in through TikTok Login Kit. The application requests the user.info.basic permission for basic profile information, subject to TikTok approval and the user's authorization.

The actual information accessed depends on the permissions granted and the features implemented in the released application.

2. How Information Is Used

Authorized profile information may be used to identify the connected account and provide sign-in functionality. TIKLIVE does not claim access to information or features that TikTok has not authorized.

3. Storage and Sharing

Information handling, storage, and sharing will depend on the implemented application features. TIKLIVE will not describe information as being stored locally, deleted, or never shared unless those practices are actually implemented and verified.

TikTok processes information under its own privacy policy and applicable terms.

4. Security

Reasonable security measures should be used to protect information handled by the application. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Your Choices

You can choose not to sign in with TikTok. You may also revoke previously granted permissions through the applicable TikTok account settings, where available.

6. Children's Privacy

TIKLIVE is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as TIKLIVE develops. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the TIKLIVE project maintainer using the contact information published on the official website.
